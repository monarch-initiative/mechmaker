  East Palestine, Ohio Train Derailment
</h1>
<div>
<div></div>
</div>
<div>
<ul>
<li>
<section>
<div>
<div>
<h2>
                EPA remains committed to protecting the health and safety of the community as the cleanup in East Palestine nears completion
      </h2>
<p>EPA personnel have been on-site since the onset of the 2023 train derailment and will remain involved as local and state partners focus on long-term environmental and health monitoring.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section>
</li>
</ul>
<div>
<div><button>1</button></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<h2><strong>September 2026 Update: </strong></h2><ul><li>The response is in a monitoring, maintenance, and reporting phase. Groundwater, surface water, and drinking water are sampled quarterly as part of the site’s long-term monitoring.</li><li>Check the <a>Documents </a>section for site-related reports and plans.</li><li>If you have questions about the response, you can email <a>R5_eastpalestine@epa.gov</a> or call 800-621-8431. </li></ul><p>See all <a>Operational Updates</a>. </p>
<section>
<div>
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>At about 8:55 PM ET on February 3, 2023, a Norfolk Southern freight train derailed in East Palestine, Ohio, about a quarter-mile west of the Ohio-Pennsylvania state line.</p>
<p>Twenty of the affected cars contained hazardous materials, including vinyl chloride, ethylene glycol, ethylhexyl acrylate, butyl acrylate and isobutylene.</p>
